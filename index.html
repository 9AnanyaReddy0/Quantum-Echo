Training Simulator

1 · Your instrument: one qubit

A classical bit is either 0 or 1. A qubit has two basis states, written |0⟩ and |1⟩, and it can also be in combinations of them.

|0⟩"zero" — the north pole
|1⟩"one" — the south pole

Every mission starts the core's qubit in one state. Your job is to steer it into the state its timeline needs.

2 · Amplitudes

Any qubit state can be written as

|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩

α and β are amplitudes. They are complex numbers, so each has a size and a direction (a phase). They are not probabilities. They can be negative or imaginary.

The amplitude panel in the puzzle chamber shows α and β live after every gate.

3 · Probabilities

If you measure the qubit you get 0 or 1. The chance of each comes from squaring the size of its amplitude:

P(0) = |α|²    P(1) = |β|²    P(0) + P(1) = 1

A single measurement result is random. Missions never roll dice: they check the state you prepared, or the probabilities that state guarantees.

4 · Superposition and the H gate

The Hadamard (H) gate turns a pole state into an equal superposition:

H|0⟩ = (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2 = |+⟩    H|1⟩ = (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2 = |−⟩

A superposition isn't the qubit "secretly being 0 or 1". It is one definite state with amplitude on both outcomes. H is its own inverse, so applying it twice brings you back.

5 · The X gate: bit flip

X swaps the two amplitudes: α ↔ β.

X|0⟩ = |1⟩    X|1⟩ = |0⟩

On |+⟩ the two amplitudes are equal, so swapping them changes nothing. On |−⟩ the swap only flips the overall sign (more on that in step 7).

6 · The Z gate and relative phase

Z leaves α alone and flips the sign of β:

Z|+⟩ = |−⟩    Z|−⟩ = |+⟩

|+⟩ and |−⟩ give exactly the same probabilities (50/50), but they are different states. The difference is the relative phase between the |0⟩ and |1⟩ parts. H can reveal it: H|+⟩ = |0⟩ but H|−⟩ = |1⟩. This is interference.

7 · Global phase is invisible

Multiplying the whole state by the same phase, such as −1, changes nothing physical. No measurement can detect it.

Z|1⟩ = −|1⟩  ≡  |1⟩

The chamber will show you labels like −|1⟩ so you can see the maths, but it counts them as equal to their target. Only the relative phase between amplitudes matters.

8 · Solving a mission

  • State circle: |0⟩ at the top, |1⟩ at the bottom, |+⟩ on the right, |−⟩ on the left. The cyan needle is your qubit. The amber marker is the target.
  • Rules: some missions limit your moves (exact or max), lock repeated gates (Echo Lock), add waypoints you must visit in order, or demand a Rewind, where you undo every gate in reverse order.
  • Tools: Undo steps back one move. Reset restarts the puzzle. Hints reveal more each time you ask. Neither ever touches your saved XP.
  • Daily board: three missions (Easy, Intermediate and Hard) rotate every day. Clear any of them to keep your streak alive. Clear all three for a +100 XP bonus.

Keyboard: X H Z fire gates · U undo · Esc closes field notes

          Phase

          Relative phase is the angle between β and α. It separates |+⟩ (0°) from |−⟩ (180°). You can't see it in a direct measurement, but H turns it into a difference in probability.

          Global phase multiplies the whole state, e.g. −|1⟩. It is physically undetectable, so the chamber treats −|1⟩ as |1⟩.

          Protocol rules

          Exact moves
          The target is checked only when you've used exactly that many moves.
          Max moves
          Reach the target within the budget.
          Echo Lock
          The same gate can't fire twice in a row. It would only cancel itself.
          Waypoints
          Visit these states in order before the target counts.
          Rewind
          After reaching the target, undo each gate in reverse order to restore the start. A wrong gate restarts the rewind.
          Probability target
          Any state with the stated measurement probability passes. No random roll is made.

          Rewards

          • Daily missions: Easy 50 XP, Intermediate 100 XP, Hard 200 XP. Each tier pays once per day.
          • Clear all three tiers on the same day: +100 XP stabilisation bonus, once per day.
          • Streak: clear at least one daily mission per calendar day. Miss a day and the streak resets.
          • Undo, reset and hints never cost saved progress.

          State circle

          The circle is a slice through the Bloch sphere. Opposite points are orthogonal (perfectly distinguishable) states. X, H and Z keep this game's states on the slice, so the needle always points somewhere on it.

          Erase all progress?

          This permanently deletes your XP, streak and daily mission records in this browser. It can't be undone.
